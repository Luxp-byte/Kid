Three color directions from Sanzo Wada's combinations

Every color below comes straight from a single numbered combination in A Dictionary of Color Combinations. Each one is shown on a phone mock of the kid's screen, so you can judge it in use rather than as loose chips. Pick a letter.

Where the colors come from. The names, hex values and combination numbers come from Matt DesLauriers' open digitization of Wada's book (github.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations). I haven't checked them against a printed copy. The book was printed in ink, so on-screen hex values are close approximations.

"Added" colors are not from Wada. They are dark inks I derived from each palette so text is readable.
