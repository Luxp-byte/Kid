Questions parents ask

Does it lock the tablet or block apps?

No. Minute Jar is a reward and timer system that makes the rules visible and fair. It doesn't control other apps. If you want hard limits too, use it alongside your device's built-in tools, such as Apple Screen Time or Google Family Link.

What ages is it for?

It works best for children aged about 3 to 10. Every task has a picture, so kids who can't read yet can still use it on their own.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Open it, type your child's name and pick a PIN.

Can two kids share one tablet?

Yes. Each child has their own jar, tasks and daily limit. They tap their name to switch.

Will my phone and my partner's phone stay in sync?

Not yet. Each device keeps its own jar. Most families use one shared tablet or the kitchen phone.

Does it need the internet?

Only the first time you open it. After you add it to your home screen, it works offline.

What if I forget my PIN?

Tap “Forgot PIN?” and answer a quick grown-up multiplication question, and you can set a new one.
